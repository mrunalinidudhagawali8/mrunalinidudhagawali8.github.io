Welcome to the first post of my new **weekly series on creative ChatGPT prompts** — exploring the future, bending the rules of reality, and asking “What if?”

---

## 🤖 ChatGPT’s Take: Job Interviews in 2040

**1. Interviews will start before you even know it.**  
Thanks to **ambient data profiling**, companies will analyze your GitHub activity, virtual meetups, and even your AI assistant’s logs—long before scheduling an interview.

**2. AI avatars will do the first screening.**  
You’ll talk to intelligent avatars that can assess **tone, behavior, ethics, and skills** in one go. Not just “what you know” but “how you think.”

**3. Goodbye whiteboards, hello simulations.**  
Think: coding during a virtual mission to Mars, or building a startup pitch in a collaborative metaverse room.

**4. Creativity questions will get wild.**
- *“If your resume were a theme park, what would the rides be?”*
- *“Design an intersection between a fridge and a road bridge.”*
- *“Pitch your portfolio as a Black Mirror episode.”*

**5. New round: The Ethics & AI Alignment Test.**  
AI will check if your decision-making is future-safe — especially for roles involving automation or governance.

**6. No more “Tell me about yourself.”**  
Instead, your **digital twin** narrates your story while you solve real-time challenges live with the hiring team.

## 💭 Why This Prompt Matters

This isn’t just fun sci-fi. Thinking this way helps:
- Strengthen **creative problem-solving**
- Prepare for **evolving tech trends**
- Reflect on **the role of humans in AI-driven workplaces**
